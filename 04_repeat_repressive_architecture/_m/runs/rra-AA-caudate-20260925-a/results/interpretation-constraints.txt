Interpretation constraints carried by this table:
  - Outcomes are genomic OVERLAP. Overlap does not imply repeat activity,
    expression, or retrotransposition competence.
  - Estimates are per 1 SD of the within-cell local SNP contribution score
    among eligible, within-domain loci; they are not PVE differences.
  - Claims exceeding `permitted_claim` are not supported by this analysis.
  - Sensitivity conjunction: adjust_cell_composition_scmd not fitted in dlpfc,hippocampus (scmd_integration_gate_fails_in_region).
  - Region gates: 2 of 3 family outcomes cleared their required region count (h3k9me3_frac 2/3 below_gate; quiescent_frac 3/3 supported; line_l1_frac 2/2 supported). This is the count that becomes 
    the run decision; an outcome below its gate is not one of them.
  - Family outcomes are gated on the BH-corrected q; the prespecified
    controls in control-outcomes.tsv are outside that family and are
    reported with raw (directional where declared) p.
  - Accessible-chromatin depletion: 3/3 regions. Concentration claim permitted.
  - Family claims rest on the primary predictor (local_snp_contribution_score_z) alone. 
    r2_pred_oof_z associations are descriptive; for line_l1_frac they are 
    near-circular and are not corroboration.
  - Specificity control: Polycomb -0.107 is OPPOSITE in sign to repressive 0.333: consistent with constitutive specificity. "Constitutive heterochromatin" permitted.
