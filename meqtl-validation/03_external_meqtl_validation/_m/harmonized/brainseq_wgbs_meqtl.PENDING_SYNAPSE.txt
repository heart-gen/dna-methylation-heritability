Status (2026-07-30): CONTROLLED ACCESS — not available for this project at present.

IMPORTANT — NOT INDEPENDENT EXTERNAL VALIDATION:
  The BrainSeq / LIBD WGBS+genotype cohort overlaps this project's discovery
  donors (AA and EA postmortem brain samples). Even if the full syn25992404
  catalog becomes available, it must NOT be treated as independent external
  meQTL validation of the VMR predictability axis. It is same-/overlapping-
  cohort evidence, closer to an alternate internal analysis than to Jaffe or
  Schulz. Regenerating BrainSeq-style cis-meQTLs on these samples is likewise
  not external validation — Phase 1 already provides within-cohort CpG meQTLs.
